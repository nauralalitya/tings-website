opening a café, a shop or a clinic means every square metre has to earn its place. we plan around your brand, your budget and the way customers move, and we can phase the build so your business keeps running while it happens.

everything begins with your story. we sit down to understand your daily routines and long-term plans, then design a home around how your family actually lives, today and ten years from now.

how we work

from the first sketch to the final cost estimate, we carry the design, so you can focus on what comes next: opening your doors, or finally moving in.

9 steps about 6–7 weeks from site visit to handover, plus revisions
01 / 09

decide what to design

where it begins

it starts with a decision: a home, a café, a boarding house or a clinic. we help you turn the idea into a clear brief.

  • not sure about size or budget yet? plan your space gives you a first estimate
  • the first conversation is free
02 / 09

space needs discussion

one or two meetings

we talk through the spaces you need and agree on what the design is locked to.

  • lock the area: the rooms you need are fitted to the floor area
  • lock the budget: the cost stays fixed, and spaces may be more compact
  • programming: which spaces, and how big each one is
  • how you live or work day to day, and the personal things that matter
03 / 09

site visit and existing conditions

2 days

we visit the site, measure it and study what is already there.

  • access, orientation, sun and wind
  • contours, existing structures and surroundings
04 / 09

schematic design in 3d

1 week

phasing, floor plans and massing, completed and presented in 3d.

  • easy to understand without reading technical drawings
  • see the space before it is built
05 / 09

design revisions

depends on you

refined until right. the number of rounds follows your needs, and we keep going until the design truly works.

  • aiming for the optimal design, not just a finished one
  • every round of feedback taken seriously
06 / 09

structural calculation

1 week

our structural engineers size the structure so the design is safe and efficient to build.

  • handled by a certified structural engineer
  • no oversized beams, no wasted budget
07 / 09

construction drawings

3 weeks

complete working drawings your contractor can build from.

  • plans, sections, elevations and details
  • material and finish schedules
08 / 09

cost estimate (rab)

1 week

a detailed bill of quantities based on ahsp, the indonesian standard unit price analysis.

  • unit prices updated to the latest year
  • a realistic budget for tendering and building
09 / 09

handover

ready to build

drawings, 3d visuals and the cost estimate are handed over, ready for permits and your contractor.

  • everything in one complete package
  • we stay reachable once construction starts
